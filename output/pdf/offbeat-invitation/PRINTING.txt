OFFBEAT - early access for the first 35 people

PURPOSE
These 35 recipients are the early-access group before OFFBEAT opens to all CBU students next week.
The invitation asks them to create profiles, share a daily song, and react to other people's picks so the feed is populated for the campus launch.
The app is not limited to 35 users. The ___ / 35 blank numbers this early-access invitation group.

FILES
offbeat-invitation-5x7.pdf: one finished invitation, 5 x 7 inches.
offbeat-invitations-us-letter-2up.pdf: two invitations on landscape US Letter, with cutting guides.
offbeat-invitation-preview.png: a high-resolution preview of the finished invitation.

PRINTING
Use 100% / Actual size. Do not choose Fit or Shrink.
The two-copy sheet is 11 x 8.5 inches (US Letter landscape).
Cut on the guides to get two 5 x 7-inch invitations.
The design has a white background and no bleed; warm white paper gives it a softer printed feel.
Write each recipient's name after Hey, and their number in the blank ___ / 35.
Both QR codes are approximately 31.75 mm square including their white margins.
Keep the QR areas unobstructed and test one physical printed copy before the full run.
